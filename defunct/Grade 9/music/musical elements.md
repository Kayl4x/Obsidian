***Music***: arranged sounds of different pitch and duration that creates musical unity

***Pitch***: how high or low sounds are (frequency)
***Duration***: how long sounds are held for.
	sounds of different durations create rhythm
***Tempo***: how fast or slow sounds are played and whether they are played at a constant, increasing or decreasing speed
***Dynamics***: how loud or soft sounds are played (volume)
***Mood***: the emotion the music evokes (happy, sad, etc)
***Timbre***: (tone quality): the specific, recognisable sound of a specific instrument
***Texture:*** How many sounds or instruments are being played together

#### Texture
1) ***Monophonic***: only one instrument
2) ***Homophonic***: main instrument with one or more supporting instruments  
3) ***Polyphonic***: many instruments together
