## Requirements

### University
University of the Witwatersrand of Johannesburg (Wits):
(Three-year Bachelor of Engineering Science in Biomedical Engineering)
- APS score of 42+
- English Home Language OR First Additional Language, Mathematics and Physical sciences level 5, with a level 6 or higher being recommended.
[https://www.wits.ac.za/course-finder/undergraduate/ebe/biomedical-engineering/]
**Does not qualify for professional registration, you still need to obtain a degree in medicine or electrical engineering or Honours in physics.

--------- 
University of Cape Town (UCT):
(Two-year Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Biomedical Engineering)
- Four-year degree in Engineering or an Honours degree in Mathematical, Physical or Computer Sciences
[https://health.uct.ac.za/biomedical-engineering/programmes/biomedical-engineering]

--------- 
Stellenbosch University:

## but, what do they even do?

### Examples of what biomedical engineers can do include:
- Create automations for continuous patient monitoring in intensive care or for people in challenging environments, like astronauts or deep divers
- Do computer modeling of blood pressure control, renal function, and nervous system circuits
- Design artificial kidneys or synthetic blood vessels
- Invent advanced therapeutic and surgical devices
- Innovate external support devices for sports medicine patients
- Find ways to better meet patient needs
- Refine medical imaging processes and equipment for more accurate diagnoses
- Improve processes for genomic testing
- Find better ways to make and administer lifesaving drugs
[https://www.mtu.edu/biomedical/department/what-is/]
