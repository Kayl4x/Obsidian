### Question 62 (3 marks) ⚠

Simplify (2m² − 2m)/m × 1/(m² − 2m + 1)

- Factorise 2m² − 2m = 2m(m − 1) ✓
- Factorise m² − 2m + 1 = (m − 1)² ✓
- 2m(m − 1)/m × 1/(m − 1)² = 2(m − 1)/(m − 1)² = **2/(m − 1)**, with m ≠ 0 and m ≠ 1 ✓

### Question 63 (4 marks)

- Let the consecutive odd numbers be x and x + 2 ✓
- x(x + 2) = 3x² − 12 ✓
- x² + 2x = 3x² − 12, so 2x² − 2x − 12 = 0, x² − x − 6 = 0, (x − 3)(x + 2) = 0, x = 3 or x = −2 ✓
- x = −2 is rejected because it is even. **The smaller number is 3** ✓

Check: 3 × 5 = 15 and 3(3)² − 12 = 15.
