Another point, of my own this time, is that suicide is by far less effort. A main characteristic of depression and melancholy is the lack of energy/motivation/drive. Living requires a _lot_ of energy. Death requires none. Death is the only end to our eternal slavery to life. To die is a certain 0 input=0 output situation, whereas life is a large amount of input, often fully what we can manage, and the output is variable. Often times the output is far less than our input, thus we become sad or depressed. Sometimes it is also higher, but in my experience, rarely high enough to cancel out the low outputs.
