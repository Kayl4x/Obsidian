I was thinking about suicide quite a bit during one of the aforementioned deficits, not seriously at least, but it was an interesting topic the main argument against suicide that appeals to me is that suicide is irrational due to its probability. In suicide your experience in life ends, thus it does not get better; whereas in life your experience may get better or worse, potentially to their extremes. Thus, suicide is probabilistically the worse option. Now while this is a fair argument, I would like to counter that life may not get better, and in many cases, it almost certainly will not. i.e. a chronic illness or seriously shit condition. A specific example is being trans. Dysphoria doesn't ever really go away, at least fully. HRT, SRS, whatever only decrease and manage dysphoria. For some individuals, being alive will be much more painful than not experiencing at all.
